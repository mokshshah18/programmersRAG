    Retrieval and context

    0.05 0.90 0.25

    A unit enters the context when its probability of being relevant reaches τ. The threshold follows from the cost of the two possible errors: τ = cfp / (cfp + cfn), where cfp is the cost of an irrelevant unit in the prompt and cfn the cost of a relevant unit that is missing. A lower τ retrieves more.
